Affiliation logos

meta-logo.svg is a vector conversion of assets/logometa.pdf from the user-supplied
Sharpening Tax paper assets. It identifies the author affiliation near the byline.
The design and colors are preserved. Meta's logo is a trademark of Meta Platforms;
the repository's MIT license does not grant rights to that trademark.

Font provenance and notices are in fonts/NOTICE.txt.

uw-madison-logo.svg is the unmodified horizontal color institutional logo from
UW–Madison's official web/digital logo package:
https://brand.wisc.edu/resource/uw-institutional-logos-for-web-digital-use/
https://brand.wisc.edu/content/uploads/2023/10/uw-institutional-logos-web-digital.zip

nyu-logo.png is the NYU logo as used in the user-requested DyT affiliation row:
https://jiachenzhu.github.io/DyT/webpage_assets/logos/nyu.png

stanford-logo.png is the unmodified cardinal wordmark from Stanford's identity site:
https://identity.stanford.edu/visual-identity/stanford-logos/wordmarks/
https://identity.stanford.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2020/06/wordmark-nospace-red.png

These marks identify the authors' institutional affiliations. They remain the
property of their respective institutions and are not covered by this repository's
MIT license. Logos are served locally and retain their original colors.
